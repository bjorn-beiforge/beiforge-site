北

Two ideas in one name

Bei · find true north

Bei (北) is Chinese for north. It means knowing where you really are before deciding where to go.

Forge · build what's next

Forge is about making: shaping, testing, learning, and building again until it works.

The name brings together the Nordic and Chinese influences behind the company.

Where this came from

Twenty years. Different industries. The same problem.

For 20 years I've worked inside large organizations: leading product delivery, guiding Lean-Agile transformations, building software, and consulting for some of the most complex enterprises in the world.

The industries changed. The problem didn't. Information is scattered across backlogs, documents, chats, roadmaps, and financial systems. The result is misalignment, hidden dependencies, duplicated work, and decisions made on stale pictures.

BeiForge exists to close that gap.

Bjorn Gass, founder

What we believe

Principles we build by

01

Context provides discernment

Context doesn't make decisions. It sharpens human judgment, and our tools never replace it.

02

No single view sees the whole

Good insight brings together honest partial views, so the people accountable can weigh them.

03

The gap is the signal

The difference between how you intend to create value and what's actually happening is where the truth is.

04

Start small, learn fast

Thin slices, real feedback, then repeat. Learning in weeks beats designing for a year.
